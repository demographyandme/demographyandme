# Fernando Fernandes

**Demographer** · Research in formal demography: mortality, life expectancy, and population aging.

I study how populations age and what a changing age structure means for population health, economies, and institutions. My methods are those of formal demography — decomposition, life-table analysis, and harmonizing vital statistics, census microdata, and international population databases.

## Research projects

Each project has a companion site with its figures and data.

### [Demographic transition in Brazil](https://demographyandme.github.io/2026-chapter-transition-brazil/)

Historical census benchmarks and projections showing Brazil's transition from high mortality and fertility to an aged population structure.

*Book chapter, Populações e desenvolvimento: 30 anos pensando, repensando e construindo o Brasil (2026)*

### [World population aging as a function of period demographic conditions](https://demographyandme.github.io/2023-world-population-aging/)

Period decomposition of population aging across 236 countries and areas, 1950–2100, using Preston–Himes–Eggers (1989) and UN World Population Prospects 2024.

*[Demographic Research](https://doi.org/10.4054/DemRes.2023.48.13) (2023) · Editor's Choice*

### [COVID-19 vaccination & age reporting, Brazil](https://demographyandme.github.io/covid-19-datasus-vaccine/)

A demographic audit of age reporting in Brazil's national COVID-19 vaccination database, benchmarked against census data, projections, and Sweden.

*[Demographic Research](https://doi.org/10.4054/DemRes.2023.48.28) (2023) · [Code and data](https://github.com/demographyandme/covid-19-datasus-vaccine-code)*

### [Migration estimates, Brazil 1960–1980](https://demographyandme.github.io/migration-60-70-80/)

Net migration estimates by sex, age group, and urban/rural residence for Brazilian states during 1960-1970 and 1970-1980.

*[Revista Brasileira de Estudos de População](https://doi.org/10.20947/S0102-3098a0287) (2024)*

## [Visual Demography](https://www.demographyandme.com/visual-demography/)

### [Demographic Flux](https://demographyandme.github.io/demographic-flux/)

Cohort-component Lexis simulation from observed HMD/HFD country profiles — stylized forward scenario, not recorded national history after the base year.

### [Explaining the differences in life expectancies](https://demographyandme.github.io/life-expectancy-differentials/)

Arriaga's decomposition of a difference in life expectancy into the contribution of each single year of age, drawn as two replacement fronts converging from opposite ends of the age axis.

### [Which causes account for a difference in life expectancy](https://demographyandme.github.io/life-expectancy-causes/)

Arriaga's decomposition of a difference in both-sex period life expectancy, constructed here from the Human Cause-of-death Database (HCD) reconstructed short list, into sixteen cause groups: each bar is that row's total on a nineteen-band heatmap.
